`cc_fishing` is a self-contained fishing resource. Players buy gear from an NPC, cast in defined zones, catch tier-weighted fish, sell at a dynamic-pricing pawnshop, and progress through 30 levels. Optional modules layer on nets, ocean-floor treasure hunts, daily challenges, a weekly tournament, and short-form active tournaments.

## Modules

Each module is a toggle in `shared/config.lua` under `cfg.modules`. Turn off what you don't want.

| Module | Default | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `level` | `on` | XP, level thresholds, level-gated equipment |
| `sales` | `on` | Pawnshop NPC, dynamic pricing |
| `equipment` | `on` | Shop NPC, rods, baits, hats, repair kits |
| `leaderboard` | `on` | Per-fish length leaderboards |
| `tournaments` | `on` | Weekly target-species tournament with prize pool |
| `active_tournaments` | `on` | Short daily tournaments at fixed times in mapped zones |
| `challenges` | `on` | Rotating daily challenges with cash rewards |
| `rental` | `on` | Boat rental at the fishing NPC |
| `nets` | `on` | Placeable fish nets with durability and bait |
| `treasure` | `on` | Sunken safes, treasure maps, pawnshop loot |

## NPCs

Two NPCs ship by default, both configurable:

- **Fishing NPC** — sells rods, baits, shop equipment, rents boats. Default at Cassidy Creek.
- **Pawnshop NPC** — buys fish (dynamic pricing) and treasure loot. Default in Mission Row.

Coordinates and models live in `cfg.npc` — see the [configuration guide](/cc_fishing/configuration).
